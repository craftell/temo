# Terminal navigation

## Sub-features

Select, search, view details, show help, cancel forms, and resize.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Launch an isolated config in tmux.

## Driving it with tmux

Capture list, search, form, and help. Test j/k and arrows. Type q in a field and confirm it is text. Resize. During a pending operation, confirm mutations and quit wait for its result.

## Gotchas

Use a real terminal. Update/View unit tests do not prove rendering.
